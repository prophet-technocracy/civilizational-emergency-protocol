# CEP Mini-App — Product Vision

**Status:** Early concept only. No deployment or public-authority function is implied.

## Possible modules

- **Knowledge library:** sourced explainers, evidence summaries and correction history.
- **Proposal workspace:** structured proposals, comments, revisions and decision records.
- **Deliberation tools:** moderated discussion designed for accessibility and pluralism.
- **Public-good project tracker:** scope, deliverables, funding disclosures and verification.
- **Governance dashboard:** published rules, decisions, conflicts and appeals.
- **Mission directory:** scoped initiatives with status, safeguards and evaluation.

## Explicit exclusions for early prototypes

Do not build national election systems, political identity databases, participant tracking, covert mobilisation, political persuasion profiling or financial mechanisms that purchase governance influence.

## Prototype principles

Start with public documentation and low-risk workflows. Use synthetic data for development. Complete privacy, security, accessibility and misuse reviews before collecting personal information or deploying features in sensitive settings.

## Questions before implementation

- Who is the user and what decision or task are they trying to complete?
- What is the minimum data required?
- Can the task be completed without an account?
- What happens if the tool is manipulated, unavailable or compromised?
- Who can correct, appeal, export or delete information?
- How will limitations be explained to users?
