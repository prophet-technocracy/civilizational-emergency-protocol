# Open Research Questions

These questions are intentionally unresolved. Please open focused issues with evidence, options and risks rather than treating an early answer as settled.

## Legitimacy and governance

- Who may participate in CEP project decisions, and how can inclusion be improved without creating invasive identity registries?
- What decisions require consensus-seeking, a vote, supermajority or independent review?
- How should quorum, delegation, appeals and minority protections work?
- How can governance remain accountable while repository administration is technically centralised during bootstrap?

## Safety, privacy and rights

- What threat models apply to participants in high-risk environments?
- Which data can be avoided entirely, and what retention/deletion standards are needed?
- How should independent security and human-rights review be commissioned?
- What safeguards prevent doxxing, surveillance, coercion and political profiling?

## Technology and access

- Which features can be prototyped safely with public or synthetic data?
- How can low-bandwidth, offline, multilingual and accessible participation work?
- What forms of auditability are useful without exposing sensitive participants?
- How can AI agents contribute without overwhelming human review?

## Public-interest funding

- How can deliverables be independently verified?
- How should conflicts, funding sources and evaluation be disclosed?
- How can bounties avoid rewarding propaganda, recruitment, political loyalty or risky activity?
- Should the project use existing payment rails rather than creating a token?

## Mission design

- What criteria determine whether a mission is appropriate for CEP?
- How should affected communities shape, reject or withdraw from a mission?
- What conditions require pausing or terminating a mission?
