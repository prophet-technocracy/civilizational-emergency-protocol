## 8. Rights, safety, and misuse assessment

Assess effects on dignity, equality, privacy, expression, association, access to remedy, due process, and physical/digital safety. This is issue-spotting, not legal advice.

| Threat or rights impact | Who could be affected (aggregate) | Likelihood / severity and rationale | Prevention / mitigation | Residual risk | Owner and stop condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[e.g. doxxing, coercion, exclusion, false attribution]` | `[groups]` | `low / medium / high / unknown` | `[control]` | `[what remains]` | `[role; when to pause]` |

At minimum consider: surveillance and retaliation; coercion or intimidation; doxxing and identity inference; misinformation or false authority; political or commercial capture; exclusion by language, disability, connectivity, location, or legal status; discriminatory outcomes; abuse of moderation; and risks to maintainers and sources.

### Stop / defer triggers

Pause the affected activity and obtain independent review if any of the following apply:

- the work would require collecting sensitive political or identity data without a compelling, lawful, independently reviewed need;
- foreseeable targeting, retaliation, or physical harm cannot be reduced to an acceptable level;
- source rights, consent, or legal authority are unclear for a proposed publication or operation;
- the product could be mistaken for an official institution, election, referendum, or representative body;
- a critical safety, security, or rights concern has no accountable owner or remedy;
- affected people cannot safely withdraw or contest harmful content where that is required.

## 9. Data minimization and privacy plan

| Data item / class | Purpose and necessity | Source / consent or legal basis | Sensitivity and re-identification risk | Access / storage | Retention / deletion | Public? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[item or “none collected”]` | `[why essential; alternatives]` | `[documented basis]` | `[risk]` | `[controls]` | `[period and deletion method]` | `[yes/no]` |

- **Default:** collect no personal or political-participation data. Prefer public, aggregate, synthetic, or non-identifying data.
- Do not treat hashing, pseudonyms, or generalization as a guarantee of anonymity.
- Do not collect identity documents, precise location, private correspondence, biometrics, or political affiliations without a separate, compelling review and explicit authorization.
- State hosting jurisdiction, processors, access roles, encryption, backups, deletion limits, and breach response before any personal data is collected.
- Do not use inactivity or an unverified signal to infer death, consent, or withdrawal.

## Completion checklist

- [ ] The proposal has a bounded question, scope, non-goals, owner, and stop path.
- [ ] Sources and claim types are traceable; uncertainty and counterevidence are recorded.
- [ ] “Do nothing” and lower-risk alternatives were considered.
- [ ] Rights, privacy, security, misuse, accessibility, and operational risks have named reviewers and owners.
- [ ] No unnecessary sensitive personal or political data is collected or published.
- [ ] Affected-community consultation is designed safely and not misrepresented as a mandate.
- [ ] External authority, permissions, and publication rights are documented or explicitly absent.
- [ ] Independent human review and any required community review are recorded.
- [ ] Reviewers can recommend narrowing, pausing, or not building.

**Passing this checklist is not a safety certification or public authorization.**
