# CEP Roadmap

**Status:** Initial proposal; priorities and sequencing are open to community review.

## Phase 0 — Foundations (now)

- Publish the charter, vision, governance draft and contribution rules.
- Establish transparent issue labels and a public decision log.
- Invite critical review from different disciplines and perspectives.
- Define security, privacy, accessibility and documentation standards.
- Identify what should not be built.

**Exit condition:** the community has reviewed the foundational drafts and documented major disagreements and unresolved questions.

## Phase 1 — Research and design

- Map use cases and stakeholders without collecting sensitive personal data.
- Compare existing civic technology and deliberation methods.
- Develop threat models, privacy impact assessments and accessibility requirements.
- Draft a modular platform specification and evaluation criteria.
- Create small, independently reviewable research tasks.

**Exit condition:** requirements, risks and limitations are documented and independently challenged.

## Phase 2 — Small, safe prototypes

- Prototype non-sensitive features such as public documentation, proposal tracking and transparent project reporting.
- Use synthetic or public data for testing.
- Conduct usability, security and accessibility reviews.
- Publish code, tests, limitations and deployment instructions.

**Exit condition:** a prototype demonstrates usefulness without requiring risky personal-data collection or implying public authority.

## Phase 3 — Community governance pilot

- Test transparent proposal, deliberation and decision procedures within the CEP project itself.
- Publish participation rules, conflict-of-interest disclosures and appeal routes.
- Evaluate representation gaps, capture risks and moderation fairness.
- Revise governance rules through an announced ratification process.

**Exit condition:** an accountable, reviewable community process is operating and its limits are clearly stated.

## Phase 4 — Carefully scoped missions

- Accept missions only with clear scope, affected-community input, safety review and measurable public benefit.
- Keep constitutional and political outcomes open.
- Separate civic research from campaigning and party organisation.
- Evaluate results independently and publish lessons.

**Exit condition:** each mission has its own legitimate participants, safeguards, oversight and exit criteria.

## Non-goals

CEP will not rush into national voting infrastructure, identity registries, political profiling, financial tokens that buy influence, or claims of representing a population. High-impact deployment requires independent legal, security, human-rights and community review.
