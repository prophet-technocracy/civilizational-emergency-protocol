# CEP Missions

A mission is a bounded piece of work with a clear public-interest purpose, accountable owners, review criteria and a safe completion path.

## Mission proposal template

Every mission proposal should state:

1. **ID and title**
2. **Problem statement** — what need is being addressed, and for whom?
3. **Scope and non-goals** — what is included and excluded?
4. **Affected communities** — how will they be consulted safely and meaningfully?
5. **Evidence and assumptions** — sources, uncertainties and competing views.
6. **Deliverables** — concrete outputs that can be reviewed.
7. **Risks and safeguards** — privacy, security, human rights, accessibility and misuse.
8. **Resources and conflicts** — funding, dependencies and interests.
9. **Acceptance criteria** — how will completion be verified?
10. **Stewardship and exit** — who maintains the result, and how can it be stopped or handed over?

## Mission lifecycle

Draft → open review → risk review → community prioritisation → implementation → independent evaluation → close, maintain or retire.

A mission is not authorised to represent a population or exercise public power merely because it is listed here. High-impact missions need affected-community legitimacy and appropriate independent oversight.

## Current mission status

No mission is approved or operational by virtue of this template. Country-specific proposals, including any Iran-focused work, must remain open to public scrutiny and must not assume a predetermined political or constitutional outcome.
